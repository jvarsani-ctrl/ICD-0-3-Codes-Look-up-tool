# Group 11 evidence — XIf7 cervix, plus vulva, vagina, uterine corpus and other female genital sites (XIf10)

Part `XIf7_female_genital`. Written 8 October 2026 by one evidence agent under the registrar's six rules for the Group 11 build. The machine-readable form is `XIf7_female_genital.json` in this folder; this file carries the same content. Every quotation below was checked against the text file on the cited PDF page; quotations marked "table image" were read from the PDF page image.

**What this part finds, in short.** Wohlmuth 2021, a SEER-18 series of girls aged 0 to 18, is this part's paediatric source (added 8 October 2026; section 11). Sarcomas, mostly embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma, are the commonest malignancy of the vagina, cervix, uterus and vulva, and are referred to Group IX. Yolk sac tumour is second at the vagina and choriocarcinoma occurs at the uterus; both are referred to Group X. Among carcinomas, clear cell adenocarcinoma is 15 of 16 cervical adenocarcinomas, adenocarcinoma occurs at the cervix and uterus, and squamous cell carcinoma occurs at the vulva: each is an option on the histology screen at those sites. Clear cell adenocarcinoma of the vagina still rests on a reference title in ICCR Vagina (Herbst and Scully 1970, vaginal adenocarcinoma in adolescence, six of seven clear cell). Pfister 2022 Table 3 lists only benign lower female genital tumours (Müllerian papilloma, mesonephric remnants, condyloma acuminatum). Squamous cell carcinoma of the cervix and vagina, gastric-type adenocarcinoma and every typed endometrial carcinoma remain picker entries until a paediatric source promotes them (data gap 1, registrar question 4). Three codes the WHO prints are absent from ICD-O-3.2: 8483 and 8484 (cervix, vagina) and 9113 (corpus, found by this part). Carcinosarcoma, adenosarcoma, myoepithelial carcinoma and mesonephric adenocarcinoma are carcinomas in the WHO scheme and Group XII in ICCC-2017.

## 3 The pathway

Node prefix `fg_`. The general carcinoma tree is written once for this site family and instantiated per site (vulva, vagina, cervix, uterus, other female genital): every option carries a `sites` tag in the JSON, and an instantiation shows only the options whose tag includes its site. `fg_site` is optional and is needed only if the router does not already fix the site.

### `fg_site` (site_resolution)

**Question:** Which part of the female genital tract does the report name as the site of the tumour?

**Hint:** Use the site the report says the tumour began in. ### `fg_scc_hpv` (ihc_checklist)

**Question:** Which p16 or human papillomavirus result does the report give for the squamous cell carcinoma?

**Hint:** Read the result as the report words it. Only block-type (diffuse, strong) p16 staining counts as positive; patchy or mosaic staining counts as negative. Sources: ICCR Cervix 2023, PDF p. 29 ("which is not in keeping with a high risk HPV-associated neoplasm"); ICCR Vulva 2023, PDF p. 16 ("p16 staining should not be reported simply as positive").

*Build note:* Rules in ihc_rules, panel fg_scc_hpv, first match wins in the order listed.

Markers: p16 (block-type (diffuse, strong) staining, negative, patchy or mosaic staining, not done or not reported); high-risk human papillomavirus test (in situ hybridisation or polymerase chain reaction) (detected, not detected, not done or not reported); p53 (mutation-type (aberrant) staining, wild-type staining, not done or not reported).

Fallback: 8070/3, basis 7. Recorded as Squamous cell carcinoma, NOS (8070/3). The WHO accepts squamous cell carcinoma NOS when p16 or HPV status is not known. Source: ICCR Cervix 2023, PDF p. 17 ("a diagnosis of SCC not otherwise specified (NOS), without differentiating the two categories").

The rules are in section 4.

## 9 Conflicts with other groups

Handled as Group 10 handled them: each code is checked in ICCC-2017 at each site of this part, a code ICCC places in another group is referred, and an alias another group already holds is listed so the site check can be tested in the browser.

- **Group 12** (8980, 8950, 8933, 8982, 8930): The WHO 2020 classes carcinosarcoma (and malignant mixed Müllerian tumour) as an epithelial tumour of the cervix, vagina and corpus, and lists adenosarcoma and myoepithelial carcinoma at these sites; ICCC-2017 places 8930-8935, 8950, 8980-8982 in XIIa4 at every site. The tree refers them to Group XII. Endometrial stromal sarcoma (8930) is in the same range, so it is referred to Group XII and not to Group IX.
- **Group 12** (9110): Mesonephric adenocarcinoma is an HPV-independent adenocarcinoma of the cervix in the WHO 2020, but ICCC-2017 places 9110 in XIIa6 at every site. The tree refers it to Group XII. Mesonephric-like adenocarcinoma of the corpus (9113, not in ICD-O-3.2) waits on the registrar; if the registrar maps it to 9110/3, it goes to Group XII too.
- **Group 10** (9064, 9071, 9100, 9080, 9084): The cervix table lists germ cell tumours; ICCC-2017 places 9071 in Xb4, 9064 in Xb1 and 9100 in Xb5 at these sites. The tree refers malignant germ cell tumours to Group X and excludes mature teratoma and dermoid cyst. An ovarian primary (C56.9) is Group X by site.
- **Group 10** (8310, 8380, 8384): The alias index already maps 'Clear cell adenocarcinoma, NOS', 'Clear cell carcinoma, NOS', 'Endometrioid adenocarcinoma, NOS', 'Endometrioid carcinoma, NOS' and 'Adenocarcinoma, endocervical type' to Group 10 (ovary). A clerk who types them for a vaginal, cervical or uterine tumour enters Group 10 unless the search box's site check drops Group 10. Group 11 must carry these strings with site routing, and the 'Also called' row of 8310/3 and 8380/3 will list Group 10's ovarian aliases (Group 10 D21).
- **Group 9** (8900, 8910, 8890): Embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma (sarcoma botryoides) of the vagina and cervix and other sarcomas are Group IX (IXa, IXd). The alias index maps 'Sarcoma botryoides' and 'Botryoid rhabdomyosarcoma' to Group 9 already; the tree refers them.
- **Group 11** (8085, 8086, 8482, 8380, 8500, 8144, 8562, 8441, 8249, 8045, 8090, 8400, 8409, 8413): Within Group XI: ICCC-2017 places vulva NOS (C51.9) among the skin sites (XIe), where its skin list holds no range for 8085, 8086, 8482, 8380, 8500, 8144, 8562, 8441, 8249 or 8045; and the XIf10 list holds no range for 8090, 8400, 8409 or 8413 at C51.0 to C51.8. Rule 3 keeps these codes in Group XI, but the subgroup at output is undetermined, and the skin part (XIe_skin_carcinoma) also routes C51.9. Registrar question 5.

## 10 Data gaps

1. **A typed paediatric source for carcinomas of the cervix, vagina, vulva and uterus in patients aged 0 to 18: squamous cell carcinoma at the cervix and vagina, the HPV status of vulval squamous cell carcinoma, gastric-type adenocarcinoma, and the types of uterine adenocarcinoma.** Wohlmuth 2021 (SEER-18) now gives clear cell carcinoma at the cervix, adenocarcinoma at the cervix and uterus, and squamous cell carcinoma at the vulva, but it groups histology into classes (squamous cell carcinoma, adenocarcinoma, melanoma, sarcoma, germ cell tumour, other) and names no further type. Pfister 2022 Table 3 lists only benign lower female genital tumours. Squamous cell carcinoma of the cervix and vagina, gastric-type adenocarcinoma and every typed endometrial carcinoma remain picker entries. Candidate source: A paediatric series or registry report that gives ICD-O-3 histology codes by site at ages 0 to 19 (for example a SEER analysis by histology code); for the vagina, Herbst AL, Scully RE. Adenocarcinoma of the vagina in adolescence. A report of 7 cases including 6 clear-cell carcinomas (so-called mesonephromas). Cancer 1970;25(4):745-757 (ICCR Vagina reference 5)..
2. **A source for the report vocabulary of embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma (sarcoma botryoides) and yolk sac tumour of the vagina and cervix in children.** Wohlmuth 2021 now reports embryonal rhabdomyosarcoma as most of the vaginal and cervical sarcomas and yolk sac tumour as the second most common vaginal histology at ages 0 to 18, so both referrals have a site-specific paediatric source. Wohlmuth gives no report vocabulary and no recognition aid: the label's 'sarcoma botryoides' rests on ICD-O-3.2, which holds Sarcoma botryoides and Botryoid sarcoma as terms of 8910/3. Candidate source: WHO Classification of Tumours, Paediatric Tumours, 5th edition (2022), female genital tract chapter; or a vaginal yolk sac tumour or botryoid rhabdomyosarcoma case series..
3. **A WHO or ICCR source for carcinomas of the fallopian tube, broad ligament, parametrium and other C57 sites.** None of this part's files covers C57; the C57 instantiation can offer only carcinoma NOS, the shared XIf list and the corpus types. Candidate source: ICCR Ovary, Fallopian Tube and Primary Peritoneal Carcinoma Histopathology Reporting Guide..
4. **The meaning of the asterisk against 8483/2, 8484/2, 8483/3 and 8484/3 in ICCR Cervix Table 1, and whether IARC has published 8483, 8484 and 9113.** Decides whether the registrar can adopt these codes or must keep the interim 8140/3 and the held mesonephric-like case. Candidate source: IARC/WHO Committee for ICD-O updates after April 2019 (the Currency Check cites ICCR Stomach p. 15 for the asterisk's meaning)..
5. **Any serum or urine test with a diagnostic role at these sites.** No file in this part gives one, so no laboratory screen is built; alpha-fetoprotein for a vaginal yolk sac tumour belongs to Group X.
6. **The ICD-O-3 topography of a carcinoma of the Skene (paraurethral) gland that presents as a vaginal tumour.** ICCR Vagina lists it as a vaginal carcinoma; ICD-O may place the paraurethral gland in the urinary tract. Both sites are XIf10, so only the topography differs. Candidate source: ICD-O-3.2 topography index..
7. **A paediatric source for 8010/3 Carcinoma, NOS on fg_histology and fg_cytology.** The option is the fallback for a report that names a carcinoma and no type, and rests on the ICD-O-3.1 manual, not on a paediatric report. Wohlmuth 2021 counts an 'other subtypes/unspecified' class across all sites and gives no site-level figure. Left as an option; the controller decides.
8. **Counts by histology and site in Wohlmuth 2021.** The text names squamous cell carcinoma and melanoma at the vulva, and adenocarcinoma and choriocarcinoma at the uterus, without counts; the distribution is in Figure 1, which is an image. The Figure 3 legend ('The survival curves of adenocarcinomas and germ-cell tumors are overlapping', panel E, the vagina) implies vaginal adenocarcinomas at ages 0 to 18, but the text gives no type, so no vaginal option was changed. Candidate source: Wohlmuth 2021 Figure 1 and Figure 3E (PMC7918615)..
9. **Carry Wohlmuth 2021's report of vulval melanoma at ages 0 to 18 into the XId melanoma part.** This part routes melanoma to the XId tree (mel_site) and builds no 8720/3 option; the paediatric report for the vulva is recorded here as an entity only. Candidate source: Wohlmuth 2021, PMC section 11..
