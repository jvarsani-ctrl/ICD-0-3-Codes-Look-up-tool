## 2 Entities

Paediatric evidence is thin for this part. Pfister 2022 (PDF p. 14) lists six malignant head and neck tumours of children: mucoepidermoid carcinoma, acinic cell carcinoma, sialoblastoma, nasopharyngeal carcinoma, NUT carcinoma and melanotic neuroectodermal tumour of infancy. No other source of this part reports a carcinoma of these sites in children. Squamous cell carcinoma is an answer on indirect evidence (Fanconi anaemia raises the risk of oral cancer, ICCR Oral Cavity 2024, PDF p. 13; Fanconi anaemia is a childhood cancer predisposition syndrome, Pfister 2022, PDF p. 19; laryngeal papillomatosis affects children and transforms to squamous cell carcinoma in a few cases, Zidar 2022, PDF p. 6). Its WHO subtypes are answers only as subdivisions of it (registrar question 1).

## 10 Data gaps

- **Paediatric occurrence (ages 0 to 18) of squamous cell carcinoma, and of its WHO subtypes, of the lip, oral cavity, pharynx, larynx, trachea, nasal cavity and sinuses.** No source of this part gives a paediatric series. Squamous cell carcinoma is an answer on indirect evidence only (Fanconi anaemia, laryngeal papillomatosis); the subtypes are answers as subdivisions. Candidate: WHO Classification of Paediatric Tumours, 5th ed. (2022), head and neck chapter; Kutler DI et al., Fanconi anemia and head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, Arch Otolaryngol Head Neck Surg 2003; a SEER-based study of paediatric head and neck carcinoma.
- **Paediatric occurrence of SWI/SNF complex-deficient sinonasal carcinoma, sinonasal undifferentiated carcinoma, sinonasal lymphoepithelial carcinoma, intestinal-type adenocarcinoma and head and neck neuroendocrine carcinoma.** Without it they stay picker entries. Candidate: Agaimy A et al., SMARCB1 (INI1)-deficient sinonasal carcinoma series, Am J Surg Pathol 2017; WHO Classification of Head and Neck Tumours, 5th ed. (2024), sinonasal chapter.
- **The WHO 5th edition head and neck volume's own ICD-O code list for SWI/SNF complex-deficient sinonasal carcinoma (8044/3), carcinoma cuniculatum (8051/3) and HPV-related multiphenotypic sinonasal carcinoma (8483/3), and any IARC ICD-O committee update that adds their terms.** ICD-O-3.2 prints no term for the first two and no code for the third (registrar questions 2, 3 and 6). Candidate: WHO Classification of Head and Neck Tumours, 5th ed. (2024); IARC/WHO Committee for ICD-O new codes lists (2019 onward).
- **A WHO 5th edition source for carcinoma of the middle ear (C30.1).** None of this part's files covers the ear; C30.1 uses the generic pharynx and larynx screen. Candidate: Thompson LDR et al., Update from the 5th Edition of the WHO Classification of Head and Neck Tumors: Tumours of the Ear, Head Neck Pathol 2022.
- **Whether the WHO 5th edition places base of tongue (C01.9), lingual tonsil (C02.4), soft palate (C05.1) and uvula (C05.2) in the oropharynx chapter.** Decides whether these sites take the oral screens or the oropharynx screens with HPV-associated squamous cell carcinoma. Candidate: ICCR Carcinomas of the Oropharynx and Nasopharynx dataset (already held by the XIc part); Lewis JS Jr, Head Neck Pathol 2022 oropharynx update.
- **An ICD-O-3.2 code for sinonasal glomangiopericytoma and for congenital granular cell epulis.** ICD-O-3.2 deleted 9150 and prints no epulis term; both are answered as not registrable. Candidate: WHO Classification of Head and Neck Tumours, 5th ed. (2024).
- **A serum or urine test with a diagnostic role at these sites (Visser 2023 Table 4 allows chromogranin A for a neuroendocrine tumour).** No source of this part names one, so no marker answer is built on the evidence gate.

## Questions for the registrar

1. Squamous cell carcinoma of the mouth, pharynx, larynx, trachea, nose and sinuses has no paediatric report in this part's sources, only indirect evidence (Fanconi anaemia; laryngeal papillomatosis). Keep squamous cell carcinoma (8070/3) and its WHO subtypes as answers, or move them to the long-tail picker?
2. The WHO table gives SWI/SNF complex-deficient sinonasal carcinoma 8044/3. ICD-O-3.2 prints 8044/3 as Small cell carcinoma, intermediate cell, with a related ovarian term, and no term for the sinonasal entity. Build it on 8044/3?
3. HPV-related multiphenotypic sinonasal carcinoma: the ICCR prints 8483/3, which ICD-O-3.2 does not hold. It is not built. Which code, if any, does the registry use?
4. Teratocarcinosarcoma (9081/3): ICCC-2017 places it at the nose and sinuses in Group X (Xb2), though its germ cell markers are negative and the WHO places it among sinonasal carcinomas. Refer it to Group X as ICCC-2017 says?
5. Rule K and HPV: when a diagnosis line names a squamous subtype and an HPV status together (for example non-keratinising squamous cell carcinoma, HPV-associated, of the nasal cavity), record the WHO subtype (8072/3) or the higher code 8085/3?
6. Carcinoma cuniculatum takes 8051/3 in the WHO table; ICD-O-3.2 prints no term for it. Accept 8051/3?
7. Middle ear (C30.1) has no source in this part; it uses the pharynx and larynx screens. Accept, or wait for the WHO ear chapter?
8. Base of tongue (C01.9), lingual tonsil (C02.4), soft palate (C05.1) and uvula (C05.2): route them to the oral screens or to the XIc part's oropharynx screens?
