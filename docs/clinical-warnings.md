**the node — Dendritic / stromal markers**

> ⚠️ A spindled or epithelioid tumour with admixed small lymphocytes and **no plasma cells** should have CD21, CD23, CD35 or clusterin requested before it is signed out as a sarcoma of unknown type. Without them 9758 is systematically missed.


### g2_histiocytic · g2hist_ecd

**the node — Erdheim-Chester**

> ⚠️ **BRAF V600E is present in about 50–69% of Erdheim-Chester disease and is treatment-changing** (vemurafenib). **VE1 immunostaining alone is not sufficiently sensitive or specific** — the assay must detect below a **10% mutant allele fraction**. VE1 is not useless: BRAF V600E can be demonstrated by immunostaining rather than sequencing, and in LCH, where MAPK mutations are found in most cases, it may aid difficult diagnoses. It is insufficient *alone*. **Record which assay was used.**


### g2_histiocytic · g2hist_alk_histiocytosis

**the node — ALK clone**

> ⚠️ **Do not read the ALK (D5F3) clone in a histiocytic lesion — it stains normal macrophages** (Goyal 2026). This **contradicts the ALK recommendation made elsewhere in this pathway** for anaplastic large cell lymphoma, where D5F3 is acceptable. In a histiocytic lesion use **ALK-1 or 5A4**, and consider molecular confirmation of the rearrangement — most often **KIF5B::ALK**.

**option: **ALK-1 or 5A4** (or another clone that is not D5F3) — positive, with at least one histiocytic marker, and the lesion shows **no high-grade atypia****

> ⚠️ The **systemic multisystem form occurs in infants**, with liver, spleen and/or marrow involvement, and can involve skin and CNS — squarely in this registry's age range. It is **ALK-inhibitor-responsive**, so a missed diagnosis has a direct treatment cost.


### g2_histiocytic · g2hist_uncoded_exit

**the node — Named uncoded entity**

> ⚠️ **Rosai-Dorfman disease is now a named WHO entity**, on the strength of MAPK-pathway mutations showing it is neoplastic. It still exits as non-reportable — but the **reason has changed**. It is **a neoplasm the code vocabulary cannot express**, not a reactive condition that is not cancer.

**the node — Skin mast cell lineage**

> ⚠️ Aberrant **CD25 / CD30 / CD2** on skin mast cells does **not** make the case systemic. It is a reason to examine the **marrow**, and only the marrow can produce 9741.


### g2_mastcell · g2mast_smear_count

**the node — Mast cells on the smear**

> ⚠️ Mast cell leukaemia is exceptionally rare in children. Confirm the mast cell percentage on the smear independently and flag for senior pathology review before finalising.


### g2_mastcell · g2mast_sarcoma_gate

**the node — KIT D816V**

> ⚠️ **Inverted rule.** A positive **KIT D816V** argues **against** mast cell sarcoma and points back to systemic mastocytosis. Almost every other marker rule in this tool reads 'positive supports' — this one does not.


### g2_mastcell · g2mast_ahn_gate

**the node — Associated haematological neoplasm**

> ⚠️ A basal serum tryptase ≥20 ng/mL **does not count** as a minor criterion when an **associated myeloid neoplasm** is present — the myeloid neoplasm raises the tryptase on its own. A case with one is **SM-AHN**, which has no code of its own: record the associated neoplasm in free text.


### g2_mastcell · g2mast_criteria_set

**the node — Criteria set applied**

> ⚠️ This registry adopts the **harmonized 2024** criteria: **1 major + 1 minor, or ≥3 minor**. **ICC** treats the major criterion **alone** as sufficient — a live conflict, and Valent's panel recommends against it. A case resting on the major criterion alone is recorded as **provisional**, not as established systemic mastocytosis.


### g2_mastcell · g2mast_tryptase

**the node — Basal serum tryptase**

> ⚠️ Two caveats decide whether this value counts. It **does not count** as a minor criterion when an **associated myeloid neoplasm** is present. And in **hereditary alpha-tryptasaemia** the value must be divided by (1 + extra **TPSAB1** copies) before the 20 ng/mL threshold is applied.


### g2_mastcell · g2mast_sm_criteria

**the node — Systemic mastocytosis criteria**

> ⚠️ **CD30 is a minor criterion in its own right**, and it is new. A legacy **CD25-only** panel misses **well-differentiated systemic mastocytosis** entirely, in which the mast cells are characteristically CD25-negative — a CD25-negative result on a CD25-only panel is **not** a negative criterion.


### g2_mastcell · g2mast_sm_variant

**the node — Systemic mastocytosis variant**

> ⚠️ 9741 names an **indolent** disease. An aggressive variant, an associated haematological neoplasm or a sarcoma coded here **understates the behaviour** — the free-text entity is what tells the registry what actually happened.


### g2_mastcell · g2mast_unresolved

**the node — Criteria not met**

> ⚠️ **Do not code 9741 from here.** 9741 requires a bone marrow meeting **1 major + 1 minor, or ≥3 minor** harmonized criteria. Nothing on this node meets that.
